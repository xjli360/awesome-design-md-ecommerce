description: |-
  The Spezia Narrow Bold headlines compress horizontally until they sit on the page like copy stamped on a garment label — efficient, self-assured, occupying less real estate than a conventional face at the same cap height — and that compression sets the tone for every design decision downstream. Bonobos runs a lean visual language where a single teal (#00799c, the explicit meta theme-color) handles all primary CTA work, dark charcoal (#232323) anchors nav text and body copy, and a warm linen (#e8e1d7) surfaces as the editorial background note that flags campaign content without importing a new palette. Type is the brand's most expressive resource: Spezia Regular, Medium, Bold, and Narrow Bold stack four weight voices in one family, then gain two more registers in Spezia Serif Italic — used sparingly in seasonal pull-quotes where warmth is needed without a typeface change — and Spezia SemiMono Medium, which handles every price string and size code, its tabular figures locking numerals into rigid horizontal alignment across dense product grids. Primary buttons use `{rounded.none}` — architectural directness, no softening — while product cards relax marginally to `{rounded.xs}`, the only concession to approachability in an otherwise grid-strict layout. The orange-red sale signal (#d72b00) sits at the far end of the chroma scale from the teal primary, unmissable against white canvas without shouting; a warm peach (#eca37d) and mid-orange (#e36d2b) layer in as graphic accent fills on illustration and campaign imagery, reminding the viewer this is still a color brand built on washed chinos and seasonal palettes. Navigation deploys a mega-menu in Spezia Bold category headers over three to four subcategory columns, with a linen-background editorial panel on the right column; below, a hairline-bordered 64px nav row carries no shadow and no backdrop blur — magazine restraint that distinguishes the brand from the drop-shadow-heavy DTC mainstream.

## Components

### Buttons

**`button-primary`** — Bonobos teal (#00799c) on a square-cornered (`{rounded.none}`) 48px-high block with uppercase Spezia Medium at 1px letter-spacing; the uppercase treatment gives the label assertive presence without adding font weight. Active/hover darkens to #005f7a; disabled fades to a pale #a8d5e2 while keeping white text legible. A `button-sale` variant substitutes sale red (#d72b00) for the teal fill and appears on clearance pages and time-limited promotional events.

**`button-secondary`** — White fill with a full 1px ink border rather than an implied ghost: Bonobos draws the container explicitly. Same 48px height and uppercase Spezia Medium label as the primary. Use on white canvas sections; on colored or editorial backgrounds switch to `button-ghost`, which replaces the ink border with a hairline (#e5e5e5) border to reduce visual weight without losing the drawn silhouette.

### Navigation

**`nav-bar`** — Single-row bar at 64px height with a 1px hairline bottom border and no drop shadow or backdrop blur — the restraint of a magazine masthead. Logo anchors center-left; category links use `{typography.nav-label}` (Spezia Medium 14px, uppercase, 0.5px tracking) and trigger the mega-menu on hover; cart and account icons sit far right as 20px SVGs.

**`nav-mega-menu`** — Full-width flyout separated from the nav bar by a 1px hairline top border. Category headers in `{typography.nav-category}` (Spezia Bold 14px) organize subcategory links in three to five columns; a right-side editorial panel uses `{colors.surface-linen}` as its background and hosts a seasonal campaign image with Spezia Serif Italic copy — the one place the serif voice enters the navigation system.

**`promo-banner`** — A 36px ink-fill strip pinned above the nav for sitewide offers. `{typography.caption}` in white, center-aligned, no close button on desktop.

### Product Card

**`product-card`** — A 3:4 aspect-ratio product image with `{rounded.xs}` corners followed by a tight text stack: product name in `{typography.body-sm}`, then price in `{typography.price-display}` (Spezia SemiMono Medium, tabular figures). Color swatches sit below the name as 20px circles with a 2px ink ring on the selected state. Sale items overlay a `sale-badge` at the top-left of the image; new arrivals use `new-badge` in the same position.

**`price-display`** — Spezia SemiMono Medium at 16px keeps numerals tabular across every grid column, a deliberate typographic choice that accelerates price scanning in dense listings. Sale price renders in `{colors.sale}` (#d72b00) with the original price to its left struck through in `{colors.muted}`.

**`size-selector`** — Square-cornered 40px tiles (minimum 48px wide) in `{typography.size-label}`. Unselected: white fill, hairline border. Selected: ink fill, white text. Unavailable sizes receive a soft gray fill with muted text and no pointer cursor; a diagonal strikethrough line is recommended but not confirmed from extraction.

**`color-swatch`** — 20px circular swatches with `{rounded.full}`. Selected swatch: 2px ink ring offset by 2px. Unselected: 1px hairline border. Swatches arranged in a horizontal row with `{spacing.xs}` gap; tap target expands to 32px × 32px on touch.

### Badges

**`sale-badge`** and **`new-badge`** — Square-cornered pill-free rectangles in `{typography.badge}` (Spezia Bold 11px uppercase). Sale badge: `{colors.sale}` fill. New badge: `{colors.ink}` fill. Both overlay product images at top-left with 3px vertical × 8px horizontal padding, no border-radius, no shadow.

### Hero & Editorial

**`hero-banner`** — Full-width section in warm linen (`{colors.surface-linen}`) for editorial landing modules. Headline uses `{typography.display-xl}` (Spezia Narrow Bold 60px, −1.5px letter-spacing); body paragraph uses `{typography.body-md}`. Image-forward heroes place text left-aligned over photography with no scrim — the brand trusts image contrast rather than overlays.

**`editorial-block`** — Used for brand storytelling and campaign moments. The headline switches to `{typography.editorial-serif}` (Spezia Serif Italic 28px) — the one place the serif voice appears in the content flow — while body copy stays in `{typography.body-md}`. Typically alternates image and text columns in a 50/50 split on desktop.

### Filters & Search

**`filter-pill`** — Square-cornered 36px inline filter tags for product listing pages. Inactive: white fill, hairline border, `{colors.body}` label. Active: ink fill, white label. Active filters render as a horizontal scrollable row above the product grid on mobile; on desktop they appear as a persistent left-side panel with checkboxes.

**`text-input`** — Square-cornered 48px input field with a hairline border that upgrades to a full ink border on focus. Spezia Regular 16px body text, muted placeholder. No border-radius; consistent with the button geometry language throughout the system.

### Footer

**`footer`** — Deep ink background (#1a1a1a, `{colors.ink-deep}`) with four to five link columns in `{typography.body-sm}` at `{colors.muted-soft}` (#c9c9c9). Column headers in `{typography.title-sm}` in white. Link hover: `{colors.canvas}`. Email capture field uses a white-on-dark variant of `text-input`. Social icons appear as 20px outlines in muted gray.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column product grid; hamburger nav collapses to a slide-in drawer with accordion sub-levels; filter panel becomes a bottom sheet; `display-xl` scales to 32px; hero image stacks above text |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Two-column product grid; nav bar stays single row; mega-menu remains available on tap; hero splits 50/50 |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Three- to four-column product grid; mega-menu on hover; persistent filter sidebar on PLPs; hero text and image side by side |
| Wide | > 1440px | Content centers in a ~1400px max-width container; product grid holds at four columns; hero bleeds full-width with centered content cap |

### Touch Targets

- Size-selector tiles: minimum 44px × 44px touch area regardless of visual render size
- Color swatches: tap target expands to 32px × 32px even when the swatch renders at 20px
- Nav drawer accordion rows: 52px minimum row height
- Add-to-cart button: full-width at 48px on mobile
- Filter pill row: horizontal scroll container with 16px side padding to prevent edge clipping

### Collapsing Strategy

- Mega-menu collapses to a slide-in left drawer with accordion sub-levels at mobile breakpoint
- Top-level category items become full-width drawer rows with expand/collapse chevrons
- Filter sidebar collapses to a bottom sheet triggered by a "Filter & Sort" button above the grid
- Hero module reflows to image-first, text-below on mobile with `display-md` headline cap
- Footer link columns collapse to stacked accordions with Spezia Bold 16px header rows on mobile
