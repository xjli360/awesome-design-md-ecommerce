description: |-
  Three founders who had never manufactured a watch launched Christopher Ward in 2004 on a single provocation: if the movement comes from the same Biel workshops, why should the price triple once a famous name is stamped on the dial? That founding logic is still legible in the visual language. #00a1e0 — a clean instrument-panel blue, closer to aviation HUD tones than to sapphire-counter jewellery blue — occupies every primary CTA, the wordmark, and the first colour field in every hero. It reads precise and direct rather than opulent; the palette never reaches for gold or cream. BrownPro carries the entire type system: BrownPro-Bold at 40–48px for collection headers and the marquee tagline "Premium watches within the reach of everyone", BrownPro-Regular for product copy and spec labels, BrownPro-Light for fine-print disclaimers and strap-option sub-labels. The palette has unusual tonal depth for a single-hue brand — a graduated stack from #a6e6ef (soft sky chips and icon fills) through #71cedb and #13d2ec (mid-teal hover states) to the primary #00a1e0, then descending to #007cad and #005474 for pressed and deep-active states. This optical depth substitutes for a second hue. Dark fields — footer, movement-spec panels, editorial campaign banners — use #1d2124, which lets dial photography float without a conventional white lightbox treatment. Product cards sit on #f9f9f9 with {rounded.sm} corners and a 1px #cdcdcd hairline; hover lifts the surface to {colors.surface-card} with a box-shadow rather than a colour-swap. Filter chips scroll horizontally at mobile and collapse to a sticky left rail on desktop. Movement-spec tables use BrownPro-Light at caption size alongside Consolas monospacing, evoking a precision instrument datasheet. The crown-shaped favicon echoes the literal mechanical crown used to wind and set a watch — one of the few brand marks in accessories that is simultaneously metaphorical and technically accurate.

## Components

### Buttons

**`button-primary`** — Hard-edged (`{rounded.none}`), filled with {colors.primary} (#00a1e0), white BrownPro-Bold uppercase label at 14px with 1px letter-spacing. The square geometry echoes the bevelled case angles and crown-guard cutouts of a mechanical watch case, deliberately avoiding the pill shapes common in fashion DTC. On hover the fill steps to {colors.primary-hover} (#007cad); on press to {colors.primary-active} (#005474). Disabled state fills with {colors.primary-disabled} (#a6e6ef) and keeps the white label, preserving shape legibility.

**`button-secondary`** — White canvas with a 2px {colors.primary} border and {colors.primary} uppercase text; hover inverts to a solid primary fill. Used for secondary actions ("Save to wishlist", "Compare") where the primary CTA already carries buy intent. The 2px border weight is thicker than typical 1px outlines, matching the sense of mechanical precision.

**`button-ghost`** — Transparent background with a 2px white border and white uppercase text. Deployed exclusively on dark hero surfaces ({colors.surface-dark} campaign banners and {colors.primary}-ground collection heroes) where a filled button would compete with the watch photography. On hover, the button fills white and the text becomes {colors.ink}.

### Navigation

**`nav-bar`** — 64px fixed bar on a white ({colors.canvas}) ground with a 1px {colors.hairline} bottom edge. The CW wordmark sits left in {colors.primary} blue; top-level navigation links ("Watches", "Collections", "About") sit right in {typography.nav-link} — BrownPro-Regular at 15px, not bold, keeping the header visually light against large product imagery. Account and basket icons sit at the far right. On scroll, a soft box-shadow (0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)) appears instead of a background opacity change, avoiding the jarring flash common in sticky navs. An optional announcement bar above the nav uses {colors.primary} ground with white {typography.caption} for shipping and promotion messages.

**`nav-dropdown`** — Full-width flyout panel on white ({colors.canvas}) with collection photography, category links in {typography.body-sm}, and a featured-model card at right. Shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.12). Separates editorial framing ("The C60 Trident family") from pure navigational links, giving the dropdown an editorial quality that reflects the brand's storytelling stance.

### Product Card

**`product-card`** — Flush-corner card ({rounded.none}) on {colors.surface-soft} (#f9f9f9) with a 1px {colors.hairline} border. Watch photography is square (1:1), full-bleed within the card image zone. Title renders in {typography.title-sm} — BrownPro-Bold uppercase at 16px with 0.5px letter-spacing — mirroring the engraved-text aesthetic of printed dials. Movement type and case diameter appear in {typography.caption} in {colors.body}. Price in {typography.price} at 20px bold. Hover lifts background to {colors.surface-card} with a 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.10) box-shadow; no scale transform, no border colour swap — the card grows heavier, not larger.

### Badges

**`badge-new`** — Zero-radius label chip in {colors.primary} (#00a1e0), BrownPro-Bold uppercase at 11px, 1.5px letter-spacing. Sits flush to the top-left corner of the product card image zone. **`badge-limited`** uses {colors.ink} (#212121) for scarcity signals on numbered editions. **`badge-sale`** uses {colors.status-error} (#cc0000). All three share {rounded.none} and the same padding (4px 8px) so they stack cleanly in the image corner.

### Spec Table

**`spec-table`** — Dark-ground definition table ({colors.surface-dark} / #1d2124) used on watch detail pages for movement specifications. Row labels ("POWER RESERVE", "WATER RESISTANCE", "CALIBRE") render in {typography.label-uppercase} coloured {colors.primary-light} (#71cedb) — they read as the backlit readout of an instrument panel rather than ordinary copy. Values render in {typography.spec-value}: Consolas/monospace at 13px, white, providing a deliberate precision-datasheet register distinct from the editorial BrownPro body copy. Row dividers use {colors.surface-mid} (#383d41). The monospace values align numeric characters into implied columns even without a formal grid, giving specs the visual rhythm of a watchmaker's tolerance sheet.

### Filter System

**`filter-chip`** — Pill chip (`{rounded.full}`) with 1px {colors.hairline} border on white, {typography.caption} at 13px in {colors.ink}. **`filter-chip-active`** fills {colors.primary} and removes the border. On mobile, chips form a single horizontally scrollable row above the product grid. On desktop ≥1128px the chip row is replaced by a sticky 240px left-rail panel with {typography.label-uppercase} category headings and checkbox-style toggles — the same active fill colour ({colors.primary}) carries across both treatments for visual consistency.

### Hero

**`hero-primary`** — Dark-ground hero ({colors.surface-dark}, #1d2124) with full-bleed watch photography occupying at least 50% of the layout width at desktop. Headline in {typography.display-xl} (BrownPro-Bold, 48px, −0.5px letter-spacing), a short standfirst in {typography.body-md}, and a single `button-ghost` CTA. Minimum height 560px. The dark ground means dial details — lume plots, index markers, seconds tracks — read without a white lightbox crop, treating the watch as object rather than product.

**`hero-collection`** — Blue-ground variant ({colors.primary}) for individual collection landing pages (C60, C65, Bel Canto, Moonglow). White headline in {typography.display-lg}; typically no body copy. The saturated blue forces the dial to float against hue rather than value contrast, which reads as confident brand identity rather than safe art direction.

### Footer

**`footer`** — Full-width dark block ({colors.surface-dark}) with a five-column link grid at desktop. Section headings in {typography.label-uppercase} (BrownPro-Bold, 11px, 1.5px tracking, uppercase, white); links in {typography.body-sm} coloured {colors.primary-light} (#71cedb), providing subtle differentiation from the grey link treatments common on DTC footers. Newsletter form uses `newsletter-input` — a mid-dark ({colors.surface-mid}) input field that integrates visually rather than sitting as a dropped-in light widget. Legal copy in {typography.caption} in {colors.muted-soft}.

### Swatches

**`swatch`** — 24px circular chips representing strap material and colour options. Unselected: 2px transparent border with fill visible. Selected: 2px {colors.primary} border — the blue ring is consistent with the brand's single-hue selection logic and large enough to read without a tick-mark overlay.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Single-column product grid; filter chips in horizontal scroll row above grid; nav collapses to hamburger drawer; hero height 320px; spec table scrolls horizontally; badge stacks vertically |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Two-column product grid; filter chips remain horizontal scroll; hero height 420px; nav shows top-level links, hides dropdown; breadcrumb hidden |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Three or four-column product grid; filter chips convert to sticky 240px left-rail with checkboxes; full nav with mega-dropdown panels; hero 560px |
| Wide | > 1440px | Max-width container 1440px centred; product grid stays four columns; hero photography scales to fill; spec table expands to two-column layout |

### Touch Targets

- All interactive controls minimum 44×44px on mobile and tablet
- Filter chips at `padding: 6px 16px` produce ≥44px tap height at the 16px base font
- Swatch chips expand from 24px desktop to 32px on mobile
- Nav hamburger icon: 44×44px tap zone
- CTA buttons fixed 48px height at all breakpoints

### Collapsing Strategy

- **Nav**: Logo + hamburger at mobile with full drawer; logo + top-level text links (no flyouts) at tablet; full nav with mega-dropdown at desktop
- **Filter rail**: Single horizontally scrollable chip row (no wrapping) at mobile/tablet → sticky left-rail with labelled checkbox groups at desktop
- **Hero**: Full-bleed photography at all breakpoints; text overlay anchored bottom-left at desktop shifts to bottom-centre stacked at mobile with reduced headline size
- **Spec table**: Horizontal scroll container on mobile with `-webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch`; two-column split layout at desktop ≥1440px
- **Footer**: Five columns at desktop → two-column grid at tablet → single-column accordion (collapsed sections) at mobile
