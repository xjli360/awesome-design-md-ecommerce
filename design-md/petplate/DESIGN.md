description: |-
  Steaming bowls of slow-cooked chicken and glistening veggie chunks photographed like restaurant entrées — that is PetPlate's primary design move, elevating dog food from shelf-SKU utility into something a human might want to eat. Every hero, plan-selector card, and email header leads with plated food photography rather than lifestyle shots of dogs running, which is an unusual inversion for the category. The brand's warmth flows from a signature orange — estimated near #E8703A — that appears on every primary CTA, step indicator, subscription plan highlight, and portioning badge, pulling the page together without relying on illustration or iconography overload. The canvas is clean white (#FFFFFF) with warm-tinted surface panels (#FFF9F5) that echo the food's thermal golds rather than the clinical whites common to health-positioning brands. Type is a geometric sans-serif at confident weights; headlines run heavy (700) and large to assert authority in a category historically dominated by cluttered bag copy, while body copy stays at readable 16px. Button corners are softly rounded — not pill-shaped, not sharp — sitting around 8–10px, friendly without being childlike. Subscription plan cards use a pronounced border and a subtle warm-surface fill on the selected state, reinforcing choice without anxiety. Ingredient lists, vet-formulated callouts, and USDA-sourcing badges appear as small pill chips with hairline borders, giving nutritional claims a document-grade credibility rather than a marketing-flare register. The nav is minimal: logo left, plan CTA right, no mega-menu, reflecting a brand whose conversion path is a quiz funnel rather than a browse catalog. Portion calculators and dog-profile inputs use step-progress patterns with the orange primary as the active-step color, turning a data-collection flow into something that reads like a concierge onboarding. Mobile behavior collapses the nav to a hamburger with the quiz CTA remaining sticky at the bottom of the viewport — the one interaction PetPlate never hides is the entry point to the subscription funnel.

## Components

### Buttons

**`button-primary`** — The dominant CTA shape across the funnel: 56px tall, 8px corner radius, filled with the brand orange (#E8703A) and white 700-weight label at 18px. Hover deepens to `#CF5A27` without a scale transform, keeping the page stable as users scan plan options. Used exclusively for quiz entry ("Get Started"), plan confirmation ("Claim Your Offer"), and checkout advancement — never for secondary actions.

**`button-secondary`** — Same corner radius as primary (8px) but outlined with a 2px primary-orange border and transparent fill, with the orange applied to the label instead of the surface. Hover washes the interior with `{colors.primary-light}` (#FFF0E8). Appears in feature comparison rows ("Learn More"), recipe detail drawers, and account portal secondary actions.

**`button-ghost`** — Transparent, no border, ink-colored label at 16px/700. Hover shifts label to primary orange. Used in the nav for secondary links, in FAQ toggles, and for "Skip this step" options in the quiz funnel.

**`button-sm-pill`** — Compact 36px pill (`{rounded.full}`) in primary orange, 14px/600 label. Appears on ingredient highlight chips that are also actions, on "Add to Box" items in the meal catalog, and on mobile filter toggles.

### Inputs & Forms

**`text-input`** — 52px height, 8px radius, 1px hairline border at rest. Focus state upgrades to 2px solid primary orange with no box-shadow ring — the border change alone is the focus signal, consistent with the brand's restrained approach to interactive chrome. Used in the dog-profile quiz for dog name, weight, and birthday inputs.

**`quiz-option-card`** — Large tap-target card for quiz multiple-choice (breed size, activity level, health goals). 2px hairline border at rest; selecting flips it to a 2px primary border with a pale-orange fill (`{colors.primary-light}`). Label shifts to primary orange on selection. Cards lay out 2-up on mobile and 3–4-up on desktop in a CSS grid.

### Navigation

**`nav-bar`** — 72px tall, white with a very soft bottom shadow. Logo sits left; right side holds at most two text links plus the "Get Started" `nav-cta` button in primary orange. There is no mega-menu or dropdown — PetPlate's navigation deliberately offers no browse path, routing all intent through the quiz CTA. On mobile the text links collapse and the CTA moves to a `sticky-mobile-cta` pinned to the bottom of the viewport.

### Cards & Plans

**`plan-card`** — The plan-selector grid is PetPlate's highest-stakes UI. Cards use a 2px border that becomes primary orange when selected, paired with a warm-tinted surface fill and a 4px outer glow ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px #FFF0E8`). Price renders at 32px/800 in primary orange. Each card contains a feature checklist with orange checkmarks. Cards stack vertically on mobile, 2–3 columns on desktop.

**`product-card`** — Meal recipe cards (Chicken, Beef, Turkey) use a white card with 12px radius and a 1px hairline border. Food photography takes the top half at a 4:3 ratio with slight drop-shadow treatment. Below the image: recipe name in `{typography.title-md}`, ingredient highlights as `{ingredient-badge}` chips, and an "Add" button. Hover lifts the card 2px with a deeper shadow.

### Badges & Trust Signals

**`ingredient-badge`** — Pill-shaped chips (`{rounded.full}`) with hairline border, 14px height padding, caption-weight type. Used to surface single ingredients (Real Chicken, Butternut Squash) in a scannable horizontal scroll on recipe cards. No color fill — the restraint signals information rather than promotion.

**`vet-badge`** — Blue-tinted pill (`{colors.vet-badge-light}` fill, `{colors.vet-badge}` border and text) with a shield or stethoscope icon at 14px. Appears near product headers and in the trust bar. Communicates AAFCO/vet-formulated credibility without the clinical coldness of a plain disclaimer text block.

**`trust-bar`** — A full-width band of three to five icon-plus-label pairs (Vet-Formulated, USDA-Sourced, Free Shipping, 100% Human-Grade) rendered on the warm surface soft background between the hero and first content section. Icon strokes are primary orange; labels are 13px body. The bar repeats near the footer on long pages.

### Hero & Marketing

**`hero-section`** — Split layout: left column holds headline (display-xl, 48px/800), a 16–18px subheadline, and the primary CTA button; right column is a large food photography image with a soft drop shadow, clipped at 20px radius. Background is `{colors.surface-warm}` (#FFF9F5), not white, warming the first impression. On mobile the image stacks above the copy and crops to a square.

**`portioning-calculator`** — An interactive widget embedded in the meal-planner section. A range slider with orange fill and thumb sits above a result display that shows daily portion weight in `{typography.display-md}` orange. Background is the warm surface panel with a rounded-lg container. Updates are live (no submit button) to create a consultative, personalized feel.

**`testimonial-card`** — White card with hairline border and a very subtle shadow. Five-star rating renders in `{colors.star-fill}` (primary orange). Quote in body-md/400, reviewer name and dog's name in title-sm/600. Cards run in a horizontal scroll carousel on mobile, 3-up grid on desktop.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Nav collapses to logo + hamburger; sticky CTA bar pins to viewport bottom; hero stacks image above copy; plan cards are single-column; quiz options 2-up grid; trust bar scrolls horizontally |
| Tablet | 744–1128px | Split hero layout activates; plan cards go 2-column; quiz options 3-up; nav shows one text link + CTA; testimonials 2-up |
| Desktop | 1128–1440px | Full nav with links visible; hero at max split ratio; plan cards 3-column; testimonials 3-up; portioning calculator widens with label columns |
| Wide | > 1440px | Content max-width ~1280px centered; hero image scales up; section padding increases to prevent overstretch |

### Touch Targets

- All quiz option cards minimum 48px tall, most 64–80px with padding
- Nav CTA button 40px height at minimum; sticky mobile CTA button spans full width at 56px
- Ingredient badge chips meet 32px minimum touch height via padding even though visual height is 28px
- Range slider thumb minimum 44×44px hit area via invisible overlay

### Collapsing Strategy

- Primary nav links hidden at < 744px; hamburger drawer contains nav links plus secondary links
- Trust bar transitions from flex-row to horizontal scroll carousel on mobile with left-padding peek on last item
- Plan card grid: 1 col → 2 col → 3 col breakpoints at 744px and 1024px
- Hero image: above copy on mobile (stacked), right of copy on tablet+
- Portioning calculator: single column on mobile, side-by-side slider/result layout on desktop
- Footer columns: 1 col (mobile) → 2 col (tablet) → 4 col (desktop)
